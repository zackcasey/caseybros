How It Works

Our Process

Every project follows the same proven path. First we figure out what we're building. Then we plan how to build it. Then we execute. Breaking things into three phases keeps everyone aligned before work begins and throughout the build.

01

Concepting

Understanding your vision

  • Discussing the vision: goals, budget, style, and requirements
  • Assessing feasibility: site, zoning, codes, and constraints
  • Creating the concept: preliminary designs per your vision

02

Planning

Building the roadmap

  • Developing plans: architectural and structural mockups
  • Finalization: material costs and timeline
  • Securing approvals: permits if needed

03

Execution

Bringing it to life

  • Preparation and building: laying the foundation
  • Installation: materials installed with precision
  • The final product: quality checks and your approval
